What we are building first

  • An appliance register you can add to on site, with location, class and owner
  • Next retest date calculated from the appliance class and environment, not typed in by hand
  • Reminders before retests fall due
  • A certificate you can send to the client without rebuilding anything
  • Import from your existing spreadsheet so you do not start again

What we are not building

  • No full job management or invoicing system
  • No accounts, quotes or scheduling
  • No replacement for your tester, and no advice on what it tells you
  • Nothing that decides an appliance is safe. That judgement stays with the competent person doing the test

Who this is for

Electricians and PAT testing businesses who test other people's appliances as billable work, and who currently keep those records in a spreadsheet or in the software that came with the tester. If you test a handful of appliances a year in your own office, the free spreadsheet is probably still the right answer.

Questions

My tester came with software. Why pay for this? If the software that shipped with your instrument already does what you need, keep using it. We are building for people who find it tied to one machine, awkward away from the desk, or hard to get a client certificate out of.

When will it be ready? We do not have a date yet. Founding customers get access as each piece lands, and a refund if it is taking too long for you.

What happens to my data? It is yours. You can export the whole register at any time, and we will delete it on request.
