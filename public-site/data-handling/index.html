Modest Ambitions / Data handling

Send less data, through the right channel

This is the current one-page data-handling summary for fixed-scope pilots. It describes the process actually used; it is not a claim of compliance with a particular jurisdiction. A processing agreement is available before any real data is requested.

Delivery payout reconciliation: fields needed

  • Platform and settlement ID
  • Transaction and deposit dates
  • Gross sales
  • Fees, promotions, refunds and adjustments
  • Net payout and corresponding deposit amount
  • Existing account mapping, if relevant

Transfer, storage and access

  • Transfer: a first-party encrypted upload link at audits.modestambitions.studio, issued after scope and identity checks. Do not send operational files by ordinary email.
  • In transit: TLS.
  • At rest: uploaded files use AES-256-GCM encryption; protected application fields use application-level encryption.
  • Storage location: the application and working files are hosted by Hetzner in Nuremberg, Germany.
  • Access: Duarte alone handles the files. Operator access uses a password, TOTP multi-factor authentication, lockout controls and a 20-minute session timeout.
  • Buyer access: one-use email links expire after 30 minutes; the encrypted buyer session expires after seven days.
  • Purpose: data is used only to fulfil the agreed engagement.
  • AI providers: the current process does not send operational file contents to an AI provider.

Subprocessors

  • Hetzner: application and file hosting.
  • Resend: transactional email and delivery metadata; no uploaded file contents.
  • Stripe: payment processing; no uploaded file contents.

Retention, deletion and incidents

  • Raw files and working outputs are deleted 30 days after delivery and no later than 90 days after upload.
  • You can request earlier deletion by emailing hello@modestambitions.studio.
  • Encrypted backup copies age out after 35 days and are not deliberately restored after the applicable deletion request.
  • Incident contact: hello@modestambitions.studio.

Review the processing agreement before sending real data. Obtain your own legal review before relying on this process for a particular jurisdiction or regulated category.
