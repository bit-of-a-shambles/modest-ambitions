Privacy and website analytics

Administrative Burden Ltd operates Modest Ambitions. Contact hello@modestambitions.studio about your data or to exercise your privacy rights.

Optional analytics

If you choose “Allow analytics”, Google Analytics records page visits and clicks on our purchase and email-contact links. Campaign tags tell us which offer and email step brought a visit. We do not put recipient names, email addresses or customer files into analytics.

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish browsers and measure activity. It also processes technical information about your browser, device and network. We disable advertising personalization and Google signals. Google may process data outside your country; see Google’s privacy policy.

Analytics is optional. Google’s analytics script loads only after you agree. Choosing “No thanks” leaves the offers and payment links fully usable. Use “Analytics preferences” below to change your choice. We retain your preference in this browser for up to six months. Withdrawing consent stops future collection and removes our Google Analytics cookies.

Orders and enquiries

Stripe processes payments. Our payment links include an experiment, market and, when available, campaign-step reference so we can reconcile orders with the offer purchased. These references identify a campaign, not an individual recipient. Payment records establish whether a purchase completed; visiting a thank-you page does not.

If you email us or buy a pilot, we use the details you provide to respond, fulfil the service and maintain required business records. Customer files are handled through the transfer method agreed for your pilot, not website analytics.
